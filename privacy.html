Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

wok-youtube-uploader is a personal, single-user application. It uses Google OAuth with the youtube.upload scope only to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

Data handling: the app stores its OAuth token locally on the owner's own computer. It does not collect, store on any server, sell, or share any personal data with third parties. It uses no analytics or advertising services.
